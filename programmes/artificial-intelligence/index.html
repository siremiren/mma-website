ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Understand AI and learn how to use it well.

AI can help with tasks such as organising information, developing ideas, drafting content and planning activities. Our training helps you understand what these tools can do, practise using them and learn how to check the results and recognise their limitations.

Whether you're looking to develop your own skills or arrange AI training for employees, students or a community group, we offer established workshops, courses and coaching alongside training tailored to your organisation's needs.

What you'll learn

Our AI training covers the knowledge and practical skills to help you use artificial intelligence confidently and effectively in everyday life, work and business.

Areas we teach include:

Understanding AI

Learn what artificial intelligence is, how it works in everyday terms and what different AI tools can help you do. Explore their strengths, limitations and the types of tasks they are best suited to.

Using AI Tools Effectively

Learn how to use common AI tools, give clear instructions and ask better questions. Practise writing prompts, providing useful context and refining responses to get more relevant results.

Applying AI to Practical Tasks

Explore how AI can support everyday activities such as planning, writing, organising information, developing ideas and solving problems. Learn how to identify useful applications in your own work, business or personal activities.

Using AI Responsibly

Learn how to assess AI-generated information, recognise mistakes and understand the importance of privacy and data protection. Explore when AI can be helpful, when to be cautious and why human judgement still matters.
