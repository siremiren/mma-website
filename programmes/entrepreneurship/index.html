ENTREPRENEURSHIP TRAINING

Learn how to start, build and run a business.

Whether you've got a business idea you're ready to explore or you're still figuring out where to begin, our entrepreneurship training helps you develop the knowledge and practical skills to take your next steps.

We work with individuals who want to start their own businesses, as well as organisations looking to develop entrepreneurial skills among their staff, students and communities.

What you'll learn

Starting a business takes more than having a good idea. From understanding your customers to managing your money and making sales, we teach the practical skills involved in building and running a business.

Our training covers areas including:

Entrepreneurial Mindset

Learn how to think like a business owner, identify opportunities, solve problems and make informed decisions.

Business Planning

Understand how to turn an idea into a workable plan, set clear goals and identify the steps needed to get started.

Customers, Products & Services

Learn how to identify your ideal customers, understand their needs and develop products or services people are willing to pay for.

Pricing & Business Finance

Understand the basics of pricing, costs, cash flow and managing money in a business.

Marketing & Sales

Learn how to reach potential customers, communicate the value of what you offer and turn interest into sales.

Business Systems & Operations

Explore the tools, processes and everyday activities involved in running a business effectively.

FREE BUSINESS PLANNING TOOL

Your 1-Hour Business Plan

Every business starts somewhere. If you've got an idea you'd like to explore, our free 1-Hour Business Plan can help you start putting it into words.

Through seven straightforward questions, you'll think about the problem you want to solve, who your customers are, what you'll offer, how you'll make money and how you'll find your first customers.

In around an hour, you can create a first draft of your business plan, which you can download, revisit and improve as your ideas develop.

BUSINESS BUILDER ACCELERATOR

Build the foundations of your business in six weeks.

Have a business idea you're ready to develop? Business Builder is a six-week programme that combines structured teaching, practical exercises and hands-on support to help you move towards starting your own business.

Rather than simply learning how businesses work, you'll spend six weeks developing your own, with guidance to help you make decisions, overcome challenges and take practical steps forward.

What makes Business Builder different?

Structured Learning

Work through the important stages of starting a business in a clear, manageable order, applying what you learn directly to your own business idea.

Practical Start-Up Support

Get help with the practical steps involved in starting a business, from understanding your options for business structure and registration to developing your website and setting up the tools and systems you need.

Guidance & Feedback

Ask questions, talk through challenges and receive feedback on your plans and decisions, with support tailored to your business and where you are in the process.

What you'll work towards

By the end of six weeks, our aim is to help you develop a clearer business plan, shape your offer and make meaningful progress towards starting your business.

The exact steps will depend on your circumstances. We'll help you understand your options and make informed decisions, rather than rushing you through a checklist just to say everything is complete.
