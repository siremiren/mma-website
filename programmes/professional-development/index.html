PROFESSIONAL & PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT

Improve how you communicate, lead and work with others.

From public speaking and negotiation to leadership and everyday communication, we teach practical skills that help people express ideas, make decisions and work more effectively with others.

Whether you're learning for yourself or arranging training for employees, students or a community group, we offer established workshops, courses and coaching alongside training tailored to your organisation's needs.

What you'll learn

Our training covers a range of practical skills that can make a difference in your work, your relationships with others and your everyday life.

Areas we teach include:

Communication & Presentation

Learn to explain ideas clearly, listen effectively and communicate with different audiences. Explore practical ways to prepare and deliver presentations, speak in public and make your message easier to understand.

Leadership & Management

Develop the skills to lead others, make decisions and manage responsibilities. Learn how to set clear expectations, support a team and communicate effectively as a leader or manager.

Negotiation & Working With Others

Learn how to prepare for negotiations, understand different perspectives and work towards agreements. Explore ways to handle disagreements and have productive conversations when people have different priorities.

Confidence & Personal Effectiveness

Develop the confidence to speak up, take on new responsibilities and approach unfamiliar situations. Explore practical ways to organise your work, make decisions and handle everyday challenges more effectively.

Ways to Learn

There are different ways to learn with us. You can explore our established workshops, courses and coaching, or arrange training designed around your organisation's needs.

Workshops, Courses & Coaching

Join a workshop or course to develop specific skills, or explore coaching for more individual support.

Individuals can enquire directly, while organisations can explore suitable programmes for their staff, students or participants.

Training Tailored to Your Organisation

If you're arranging training for employees, students, volunteers or a community group, we can develop learning around your objectives and the people taking part.

We'll focus on the skills that matter most to your group, using practical examples and activities relevant to their needs.
