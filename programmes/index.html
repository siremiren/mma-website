Measuring Success

How can we measure success together?

The right measures depend on the people you're training and what you want them to achieve. We can work with you to agree suitable outcomes and ways to assess progress.

Here are some examples of what we could measure:

01

Knowledge Gained

What participants understand before and after training, assessed through questions, activities or short knowledge checks.

02

Practical Skills

What participants can demonstrate through exercises, completed tasks or practical activities.

03

Learner Confidence

How confident participants feel about using their new skills, measured through feedback or before-and-after assessments.

04

Participation & Completion

Attendance, engagement and completion rates, where these are relevant to the programme's objectives.

05

Applying New Skills

How participants put their learning into practice, whether through improved communication, leadership, productivity or other practical applications.

06

Using AI in Practice

How participants apply AI tools to relevant tasks, including improvements in how they work or complete activities.

07

Entrepreneurship Progress

Progress towards agreed business-building milestones, such as testing an idea, developing a business plan or preparing to launch.

08

Feedback & Next Steps

Participant feedback, areas for improvement and opportunities for further learning or support.
