Training That Works for Your Organisation

Every organisation has different needs. Some are looking for an existing programme that covers the skills their people need. Others want training developed around specific objectives, challenges or groups.

We offer both.

Training can be delivered online or in person, depending on your organisation's needs and the programme you choose.

Established Programmes

Choose from our existing workshops, courses and coaching programmes across entrepreneurship, professional and personal development, and artificial intelligence.

Tailored Training

If your organisation has particular requirements, we can develop training around the people taking part and what you want them to achieve.

How We Work With You

Good training starts with understanding what you want to achieve and who will be learning.

Whether you're interested in an established programme or something tailored to your organisation, we'll work with you to find an approach that makes sense.

1. Understand Your Needs

We'll start with a conversation about your organisation, the people you're looking to train and what you'd like them to learn or improve.

2. Agree the Right Approach

Together, we'll explore the available options, agree on suitable training content and discuss practical arrangements for delivery.

3. Deliver Practical Training

Our sessions combine clear explanations with relevant examples, discussion and opportunities to practise. We focus on helping participants understand what they're learning and how to apply it.

4. Review the Learning

Where appropriate, we can agree ways to assess what participants have learnt, gather feedback and identify opportunities for further development.

The Experience Behind Our Training

Mind and Method Academy draws on over 15 years' experience coaching and teaching people across three continents, in settings including universities, schools, community centres and prisons.

That experience includes work delivered with or for organisations such as the University of East London, Birmingham City University and Lewisham Council.

Working with people from different backgrounds and learning environments has shaped our approach. We focus on making training clear, accessible and relevant, giving participants opportunities to understand new ideas, practise skills and apply what they've learnt.
