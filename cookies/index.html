Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Mind and Method Academy Ltd uses cookies and browser storage on its website.

1. Cookies and Browser Storage

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. Local storage is another way a website can save information in your browser.

2. How We Use Cookies

Remembering Your Choice

We save your analytics choice and when you made it in local storage (mma_consent_v2). Your choice applies to this website address and is valid for 180 days. If your browser cannot save it, you may be asked again sooner.

Optional Analytics

If you accept optional analytics, we use Google Analytics cookies to understand how people use the website and help us improve it.

3. Other Services

The website requests Google Fonts when it loads. When you submit a Contact or Workshop form, your browser connects to FormSubmit, which publishes its own privacy and cookie information.

4. Managing Your Choice

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of this Cookie Policy page to accept or reject optional analytics or change your choice. If you turn off active analytics, the page asks you to confirm a reload and warns that you may lose unsaved entries. You can also clear cookies and stored site data in your browser.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time.
