# Mind and Method Academy website audit

**Audit date** 21 November 2026, Europe/London  
**Production** [mindandmethodacademy.com](https://mindandmethodacademy.com/about/)  
**Source baseline** GitHub `main` at [`681326c4f05ccc8e228d9b8eeb0972131d4c96b3`](https://github.com/siremiren/mma-website/commit/681326c4f05ccc8e228d9b8eeb0972131d4c96b3), the merge of PR #2 at 01:18 UTC.  
**Scope** Included public pages, error and utility routes, and shared components. The homepage and Media page were excluded. The Business Plan submission and Workshop registration pauses were treated as intentional.

## 1. Executive assessment

The repositioned site presents three distinct training areas. The Programmes hub explains topics, audiences and practical outcomes, and About makes the move beyond entrepreneurship credible. The navy, cream and gold palette, typography and CTA hierarchy are generally consistent. The AI and Professional & Personal Development pages give established and tailored training comparable visual weight. Netlify served the in-scope content pages and shared assets successfully during testing.

The biggest discrepancy is Contact. The current GitHub and production versions put a Netlify form first, while the audit brief identifies email and WhatsApp as the primary route and says the form was deliberately removed. At 320 px, the visible email and WhatsApp options sit below the entire form. This is a confirmed conflict with the supplied decision. Form delivery was **Not tested**. Confirm the operative decision before making changes to Contact or its privacy disclosures.

The other immediate issues are keyboard focus disappearing behind the mobile menu, Workshop content staying invisible in a short viewport and low contrast on legal links and the WhatsApp handoff. Two outward-facing links also fail to get visitors to the intended destination. No P0 issue was identified within the review.

For qualified enquiries, the main opportunity is to give visitors enough information to choose what to ask about. Individuals can compare subject matter but cannot tell which AI or development offers are currently available and in what format. Organisations can see that tailored training is offered, but the Entrepreneurship page funnels their enquiry into Business Builder. Search fundamentals are mostly sound. Freshness is uneven in the Entrepreneurship and Workshop metadata, and the public machine-readable overview still describes a different offer. The fast sampled lab navigations and small first-party payloads point to targeted fixes before broad performance work.

## 4. Detailed page findings

### About Us

The founder portrait loads and has a useful alternative description. The three-continent experience and qualifications add context, and the origin story explains the business education roots alongside newer teaching areas. The single CTA works as a directional next step. No material page-specific copy defect was confirmed. Retest the mobile layout shift before assigning a cause (AUD-018). The owner should retain evidence for the published experience and qualifications if used in public credibility claims.

### Programmes

Linked subject headings, “What You Will Learn”, “Who This Is For”, and practical outcomes make the breadth clear. The organisation and evaluation copy explain commissioning in useful terms. Extensive stacked content makes quickly switching subjects difficult on a 320 px phone. Preserve the detail for visitors who need it and offer direct jumps to the three areas and organisational section. The source CTA links use `aria-describedby` to associate each with its card heading, but all land on the same Contact route (AUD-007). The menu and optional reveals should be fixed centrally (AUD-002 and AUD-003). [Visible programme card](MMA_WEBSITE_AUDIT_2026-11-21_evidence/programmes-script-enabled-320.png) · [Same card with scripts disabled](MMA_WEBSITE_AUDIT_2026-11-21_evidence/programmes-script-disabled-320.png)

### Entrepreneurship

The page explains practical topics including pricing, cash flow and operations, then sets out a concrete six-week programme. The wording “our aim is to help” appropriately frames outcomes. Extend the existing opening promise to organisations into the body and point visitors to the shorter Workshop (AUD-008). At 320 px the Business Builder CTA wraps to two lines in a 272×78 CSS px target, with the full label retained. Confirm which delivery formats an organisation can request before adding detail. Give this pillar description and share metadata comparable to the other two (AUD-011).

### Professional & Personal Development

The examples in communication, leadership and negotiation give the broad headline substance. Established learning and tailored work are both clearly named. “Join a workshop or course” would be more useful with a real example and its availability. If the current public offer is enquiry-led, say “Ask which communication and leadership workshops are currently available” and add format or audience where confirmed. Confirm with the owner which course, workshop and coaching options are active (AUD-007).

### Artificial Intelligence

Tool use, practical tasks and responsible use are distinct and concrete. The page welcomes people new to AI and organisations. “Join practical learning sessions” leaves it unclear whether there is a scheduled option for an individual. Add a verified example with the intended learner level, format and how to ask for availability. Keep the existing team-training option at equal prominence (AUD-007).

### Contact

The page welcomes both audiences, the fields have visible labels, the browser identifies required fields, and the organisation note gives a useful three-part enquiry prompt. That prompt could work for email or WhatsApp. The operative decision on form usage needs owner confirmation. The visible 320 px form takes precedence over the direct links. At 1440 px, the direct options have their own column but are still headed “Other ways to reach us”. By source, the script treats an HTTP success response as receipt. The Business Plan event handler ignores submissions named `contact`. Netlify notification or inbox delivery was **Not tested**. The email address is visible and actionable and the direct WhatsApp destination responded. ## 5. Shared components, technical quality and audience journeys

**Header and navigation.** The Programmes hub is a separate link from its submenu button. The submenu reports expanded state and responds to keyboard activation and Escape. At 769, 800, 900, 901 and 950 px on Programmes the desktop navigation fit and the main CTA stayed clear. The full-screen mobile menu's focus and expanded state should be addressed in shared code. Keep the nav markup aligned across duplicated pages or use a shared template when maintaining them. [Mobile menu covering the page during focus test](MMA_WEBSITE_AUDIT_2026-11-21_evidence/mobile-menu-375.png)

**Buttons, colour, spacing and motion.** Typical primary body CTAs measured about 55 px high and the Contact WhatsApp button measured 57 px with its 1 px border. Borderless gold and navy primary buttons are intentional filled variants. The measurable compression is concentrated in Workshop's narrow card, rather than a global button sizing problem. The sampled document widths showed no horizontal scrollbar, but the 320 px confirmation text overflowed its card under the global overflow clipping. The floating WhatsApp launcher can cover text in the bottom-right portion of a 320 px screen, as seen on the programme card, which scrolls clear. Shared typography uses Montserrat and Lato with readable main body sizing. Use accessible foregrounds and focus colours as shared tokens. Reduced-motion CSS shortens transitions and animations. Smooth scrolling remains set globally, and the observer still controls initial visibility. Include both in the fix for AUD-003. WCAG claims here are limited to the observed focus, state and contrast failures. Screen-reader announcement, full target-size conformance and user text-spacing overrides remain **Not tested**. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)

**Discoverability.** The ten included indexable marketing and policy pages had a single H1, self-canonical and sitemap entry. The main content pages had meaningful individual titles and descriptions except Entrepreneurship's missing description. Business Plan's `noindex` and sitemap exclusion align. The sampled slashless About redirected to its slash URL. The explicit `/programmes/index.html` also returned 200 with the same canonical-bearing HTML, giving crawlers an alternate URL with a clear preferred URL. No structured data was found on the included pages. Accurate breadcrumb markup could be added alongside visible programme breadcrumbs if that navigation is introduced. Avoid course or event details in markup until matching public offers and dates exist. Search Console crawling, actual search positions and indexing for utility routes were **Not tested**.

**Performance and maintainability.** Production sent gzip for sampled HTML and CSS, revalidation for pages, one-day caching for CSS/JS and 30 days for the portrait.
