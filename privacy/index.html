Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Mind and Method Academy Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who visit our website, register for our programmes, or otherwise engage with our services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data Controller

Mind and Method Academy Ltd
71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ
Company Registration Number: 16932969
ICO Registration Number: ZC073024
Email: hello@mindandmethodacademy.com

2. What Personal Data We Collect

  • Identity data: first name, last name
  • Contact data: email address, telephone number
  • Professional data: organisation name, job title or role
  • Enquiry data: the details and messages you send us
  • Programme data: registration details, attendance records, programme progress
  • Business Plan data: your answers and optional marketing choice
  • Technical data: our hosting service receives information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit. If you allow optional analytics, we also collect information about how you use the website.

3. How We Collect Your Data

  • When you contact us through our enquiry form, by email or on WhatsApp, including about a workshop or programme
  • When you register for or participate in one of our programmes
  • When you visit our website or choose to allow optional analytics
  • When you engage with us on social media platforms

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to enquiries
  • To deliver and administer our education programmes
  • To communicate about programme updates, schedules, and materials
  • To process payments
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

If you previously submitted a Business Plan, we may still hold the contact details and answers you provided, subject to the retention periods below. The online tool is currently paused.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent: If you choose to allow optional analytics. Permission for marketing emails is separate.
  • Contract: Where processing is necessary to provide the training or coaching you contracted for, or to take steps you request before entering a contract.
  • Legitimate interests: Where necessary to respond to enquiries, liaise with organisations or operate and secure our website, taking account of your rights and interests.
  • Legal obligation: Where we must comply with a legal requirement, such as keeping applicable tax records.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with service providers, professional advisers, and regulatory authorities where required by law.

Netlify hosts the website and processes and stores enquiries submitted through Netlify Forms. We use our email service to receive and reply to enquiries. Our previous Contact and workshop forms used FormSubmit to forward submissions to us. The paused Business Plan tool used Netlify Forms to store submissions and Resend to process plan emails.

We use Google Fonts to display type on the site. If you allow optional analytics, we use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site. See our Cookie Policy for how to manage your choice.

If you follow a link to WhatsApp or Google reviews, that service handles your interaction under its own privacy information. We use the messages you send us to respond to you.

7. Data Retention

We keep submitted Business Plans and our copies for 12 months from submission unless a separate purpose justifies keeping them for a different period. If we use a plan during paid training or coaching, we may keep it with the participant record for up to 36 months after participation ends, where needed for that purpose.

We keep general enquiry records for up to 24 months from the last substantive contact and programme participant records for up to 36 months after participation ends.

We generally keep relevant company tax records for six years from the end of the accounting period they relate to. Some records may need to be kept longer where the applicable rules require it or a documented reason justifies it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access your personal data, correct it, erase it, restrict how we use it or receive a copy in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests and can object to direct marketing at any time. To exercise your rights, email hello@mindandmethodacademy.com. We respond without undue delay and normally within one calendar month. If the law allows us to extend that time, we will explain why within the first month.

9. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data.

10. International Transfers

Some services described above can process personal data outside the UK. For example, Resend publishes that it processes and stores email data in the United States. Google and WhatsApp describe their international processing in their privacy notices. To ask about transfers involving your data, the safeguards that apply and how to obtain a copy, email hello@mindandmethodacademy.com.

11. Complaints

If you have a complaint about how we handle your personal data, we ask that you contact us first so we have the opportunity to put things right.

Stage 1 — Contact us directly. Please email hello@mindandmethodacademy.com with details of your complaint. We will acknowledge your email within 72 hours and aim to resolve the issue within 14 days of receipt.

Stage 2 — Information Commissioner's Office. If we are unable to resolve your complaint, or you remain dissatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO at any time.

Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF. ico.org.uk. Helpline: 0303 123 1113.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
