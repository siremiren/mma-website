About Us

We believe learning should be useful.

Mind and Method Academy was built around that belief. We work with individuals and organisations, drawing on years of teaching and coaching experience to make learning clearer, more practical and easier to apply in work, business and everyday life.

Why We Exist

Our beginnings still shape what we do.

Mind and Method Academy began with entrepreneurship education. We helped people understand what it takes to start a business and take practical steps towards doing it. Along the way, one thing became clear: knowing what to do and being able to do it are not always the same.

And that doesn't just apply to business. A good manager can learn to become a better leader. Someone with years of experience can develop new ways of communicating. Someone who has never used AI can learn how to make it useful in their work. There's always something new to learn, and something we can improve.

That's why our work extends beyond entrepreneurship. We want to make useful education accessible to more people, whatever their background or reason for learning. Whether we're teaching individuals or working with organisations, our aim is to help people develop skills they can put to good use.

"Learning should help you improve what you already do, or give you the skills to do something entirely new."
— What guides our work
Meet the Founder

Samuel Iremiren

Founder, Mind and Method Academy

Samuel Iremiren founded Mind and Method Academy. He brings over 15 years of coaching and teaching experience across three continents, including work in universities, schools, community centres and prisons.

He holds an MBA and an MSc in Economics, with a professional background spanning finance, technology, project management and business. He has also started and run businesses himself, so his teaching draws on both professional experience and the realities of making decisions, solving problems and getting things done.

Working with people from different backgrounds has shaped Samuel's belief that good training should be clear, practical and relevant to the person learning. That belief helped shape the Academy's early entrepreneurship programmes and continues to guide its wider work today.

Our Approach

Good training starts with the people learning.

People bring different experiences, questions and goals to a workshop. We don't expect everyone to arrive knowing the same things or learning in the same way. Our approach is built around making training understandable, welcoming and useful, whether someone is learning something for the first time or building on years of experience.

Respect different starting points

Everyone brings something different to the room. We recognise those differences, make space for questions and use examples that people from different backgrounds can relate to.

Keep things simple

We believe complex subjects can be taught in ways that are easy to understand. We use straightforward language, clear explanations and practical examples, without assuming everyone already knows the terminology.

Make learning active

Understanding something is one thing. Being able to use it is another. Through discussion, exercises and opportunities for practice, we help people explore how new skills apply to their own situations.
